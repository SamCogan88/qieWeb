Programme Quality

Programme lifecycle & review

NCI programmes are developed, validated, monitored and periodically reviewed through a structured programme lifecycle.

1

Develop

Strategic case, stakeholder consultation, curriculum and programme design.

2

Validate

Internal approval and independent external evaluation for QQI validation.

3

Deliver

Approved curriculum, learning outcomes, teaching, assessment and learner supports.

4

Monitor

Annual Programme Monitoring, evidence, feedback and approved programme changes.

5

Review & revalidate

Periodic critical review, external panel evaluation and revalidation or retirement.

Academic Governance

How academic governance works at NCI

Academic Council is the authoritative body for academic quality matters, supported by specialist sub-committees and School and Programme Committees.

Governing BodyInstitutional governance and oversight
↓
Academic CouncilAcademic quality, standards, strategy and oversight
↓
Academic Programmes & Quality CommitteeProgramme lifecycle, QAES, partnerships and quality review
Learning, Teaching & Assessment CommitteeTeaching, learning, assessment, academic integrity and enhancement
Research & Innovation CommitteeResearch strategy, quality, capacity and postgraduate research
Ethics Sub-CommitteeResearch ethics and governance
Intellectual Property Advisory CommitteeIP identification, protection and commercialisation
↓
School CommitteesSchool strategy, programme quality and staff/student experience
Programme CommitteesProgramme delivery, monitoring, assessment and periodic evaluation
Class Representative LiaisonLearner feedback on programme content, delivery, assessment and development
Academic Council delegates defined responsibilities to its sub-committees. Programme Committees sit within the School governance structure, while Class Representative Liaison provides a formal route for learner feedback into programme and School decision-making.
